Featured projects (large cards):

1. ScholarBot  (Jan 2026)
   School FAQ assistant that answers from the school's own documents.
   Only calls the LLM when the rule engine is unsure.
   Highlights:
     - Rule-based intent engine (tokenization, normalization, confidence scoring)
       with Gemini 2.5 Flash as the fallback.
     - Timetables, notices and rulebooks split into sliding-window chunks passed as LLM context.
     - Confidence threshold → most questions never hit the API.
     - Flask REST backend with NLP, PDF parsing, intent classification and LLM calls in separate modules.
   Stack: Python, Flask, Gemini API, RAG, NLP

2. PredictGrad  (Jun 2025)
   Flags engineering students at academic risk before their grades drop.
   Trained on 905 students and deployed on Streamlit.
   Highlights:
     - Regression & classification with Ridge, CatBoost, ExtraTrees and BalancedBagging (voting + stacking).
     - MAE 5.16 – 7.10 on predicted scores · F1 0.51 on imbalanced risk classification.
     - Target built only from semester 1 & 2 data — no future leakage.
     - SHAP risk dashboard showing why a student was flagged.
   Stack: Scikit-learn, CatBoost, SHAP, Streamlit
   Links: https://thepredictgrad.streamlit.app  ·  https://github.com/devpanchal2005/PredictGrad

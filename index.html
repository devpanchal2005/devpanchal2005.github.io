ScholarBot

Jan 2026

School FAQ assistant that answers from the school's own documents. It only calls the LLM when the rule engine is unsure.

  • Rule-based intent engine (tokenization, normalization, confidence scoring) with Gemini 2.5 Flash as the fallback.
  • Timetables, notices and rulebooks are split into sliding-window chunks and passed to the LLM as context.
  • A confidence threshold sends only ambiguous or out-of-distribution questions to Gemini, so most questions never hit the API.
  • Flask REST backend with NLP, PDF parsing, intent classification and LLM calls in separate modules.

PredictGrad

Jun 2025

Flags engineering students at academic risk before their grades drop. Trained on 905 students and deployed on Streamlit.

  • Regression and classification with Ridge, CatBoost, ExtraTrees and BalancedBagging, combined through Voting and Stacking.
  • MAE 5.16 to 7.10 on predicted scores, F1 0.51 on imbalanced risk classification.
  • Target built only from semester 1 and 2 data, so nothing leaks from the future.
  • Risk dashboard that uses SHAP to show why a student was flagged.
